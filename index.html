Understanding Facial Structure

Why structure matters for a natural look

01 Where Soft Meets Firm

Our faces are built in layers. Soft tissue, connective tissue and the underlying frame work together, and the places where they meet help shape how a face looks and moves. AURENTHE starts every design by looking at these layers as one connected structure.

Layered

Each layer has its own role in the overall shape.

Connected

Changes in one area are felt across the face.

Balanced

Harmony matters more than any single feature.

In Motion

A face is seen in expression, not only at rest.

Good design is not about adding more.
It is about how everything fits together.

01 Four Ideas, One Face

The AURENTHE approach looks at support, tension, anchoring and connection together. Instead of focusing on one spot, it considers how each area relates to the rest of the face, aiming for results that feel like your own.

Signature Program: Nose Contour

Designed around the center of the face

01 Shape and Softness Together

The nose sits at the center of the face, so small details change the overall impression. A good design keeps its shape while still feeling soft and natural with every expression.


The Nose Contour program looks at the nose together with the forehead, cheeks and chin, so the result fits the whole face rather than standing out on its own.

The best profile is not the sharpest one.
It is the one that suits your face.
